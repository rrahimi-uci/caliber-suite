# Customer Support Service Levels

Document ID: POL-SUP-001
Policy domain: support
Owner: Customer Support
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2026-05-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy states how quickly Support responds to customer cases and how availability
failures are compensated. It applies to customers on the Standard and Premium plans.
Enterprise customers have their service levels in their signed order form, and this policy
does not state response targets for them.

## 2. Severity levels

| Severity | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| P1 | Production is down or a core feature is unusable for all users; no workaround |
| P2 | A core feature is degraded, or unusable for some users; a workaround exists |
| P3 | A minor problem, a question, or a feature request |

The customer proposes a severity when opening a case. Support may change it with a stated
reason and tells the customer when it does.

## 3. First-response targets

| Severity | Standard plan | Premium plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | 4 business hours | 1 hour, 24x7 |
| P2 | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| P3 | 2 business days | 1 business day |

The first response is a reply from a support engineer who has read the case. An automatic
acknowledgement does not count. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 in the
customer's contracted region, excluding public holidays.

## 4. Support channels

Standard customers open cases through the Help Center and by e-mail. Premium customers can
also use the 24x7 phone line for P1 cases. Cases opened by phone are logged by the agent
who takes the call.

## 5. Availability commitment

Premium plans carry a monthly availability commitment of **99.9%**. If availability in a
calendar month is below 99.9% but at least 99.0%, the customer receives a service credit
of **10%** of that month's fee. If it is below 99.0%, the credit is **25%**. Credits are
applied to the next invoice, must be requested within 30 days of the end of the month, and
are the only remedy for downtime. Standard plans have no availability commitment.

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 5 days ahead on the status page does not count
as downtime.

## 6. Escalation

A customer who is not satisfied with progress can ask for escalation. A P1 case that has
not been resolved after 4 hours is escalated to the on-call support manager; after 8 hours
it is escalated to the Director of Support. Customers are updated at least every 2 hours
while a P1 case is open.

## 7. Closing a case

A case is closed when the customer confirms the resolution, or 7 days after Support's last
reply if the customer does not respond. A closed case can be reopened within 30 days.
