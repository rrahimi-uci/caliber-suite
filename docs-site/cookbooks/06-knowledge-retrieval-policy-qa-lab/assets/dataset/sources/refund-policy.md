# Refund and Cancellation Policy

Document ID: POL-BIL-004
Policy domain: billing
Owner: Finance Operations
Version: 3.2
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Supersedes: version 3.1 (effective 2024-09-01)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out when a customer may receive money back for a CALIBER Cloud
subscription, add-on or one-time charge, how a refund is requested, and how long it
takes to arrive. It applies to every customer on a Standard or Premium plan. Customers
on an Enterprise plan are governed by the refund terms in their signed order form, not
by this policy (see section 7).

Support agents must quote this policy, not the customer-facing FAQ, when the two differ.
The FAQ is maintained by Support Enablement and is reviewed less often than this policy.

## 2. Refund window

A customer may request a refund within **30 calendar days** of the charge date. The
window is counted from the date printed on the invoice, not from the date the payment
settled.

Version 3.1 of this policy allowed only **14 calendar days**. Version 3.2 extended the
window to 30 days for every charge made on or after **1 March 2026**. A charge made
before 1 March 2026 remains subject to the 14-day window of version 3.1, even if the
request is made after that date. Requests received after the applicable window are
declined, except where section 6 applies.

## 3. What can be refunded

| Charge type | Refundable | Amount refunded | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly subscription | Yes, inside the window | Full charge | Access ends when the refund is issued |
| Annual subscription | Yes, inside the window | Full charge | After day 30 the plan can be cancelled effective at the end of the term, with no refund |
| Add-on seats | Yes, inside the window | Pro-rated credit for unused days | Issued as account credit, not to the card |
| Usage-based overage | No | None | Usage already consumed cannot be reversed |
| One-time setup fee | No | None | Non-refundable in every case |
| Training and onboarding sessions | Yes, if cancelled 5 business days before the session | Full charge | Later cancellations are non-refundable |

## 4. How to request a refund

1. Sign in to the Billing portal.
2. Open **Account > Billing > Charges**.
3. Select the charge you want refunded.
4. Choose **Request refund**.
5. Pick a reason from the list and submit the request.

The customer receives a confirmation e-mail with a case number. Support agents must not
promise an outcome before Finance Operations has checked eligibility.

## 5. Approval and payout

Refunds of up to $5,000 are approved automatically when the charge is inside the window
and of a refundable type. A refund above $5,000 needs approval from a Finance Operations
manager, which adds up to 3 business days.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business
days. Refunds are issued in the currency of the original charge; CALIBER does not cover
exchange-rate differences. Add-on seat refunds are the one exception and are issued as
account credit.

## 6. Exceptions

Finance Operations may grant a refund outside the window when CALIBER caused a billing
error (a duplicate charge or a charge after a confirmed cancellation). The customer must
report the error within 90 days of the charge. No other exception may be granted by
Support without written approval from the Director of Finance Operations.

If a customer opens a payment dispute (chargeback) with their bank before requesting a
refund, the account is suspended until the dispute closes and the refund request is
closed without action.

## 7. Enterprise plans

Enterprise customers negotiate refund, cancellation and termination terms in their order
form. This policy does not state a refund window for Enterprise plans. Support must route
an Enterprise refund question to the customer's account manager.
