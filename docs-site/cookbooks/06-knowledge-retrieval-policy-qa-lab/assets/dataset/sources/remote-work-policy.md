# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Document ID: POL-HR-010
Policy domain: hr
Owner: People Operations
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2025-09-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy describes where and how employees may work. It applies to all employees.
Roles that need access to a physical site, such as the hardware lab, are excluded and are
set out in the job description.

## 2. Work models

- **Hybrid (default).** Employees who live within 50 miles of a CALIBER office work from
  that office at least **2 days a week**. Teams choose their anchor days.
- **Fully remote.** Employees who live more than 50 miles from an office, or whose role
  is approved as remote by their director, work from home or a co-working space.

Core hours are **10:00 to 15:00** in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should be
scheduled inside core hours.

## 3. Home-office support

| Item | Amount | Eligibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home-office stipend | $600, paid once | After 90 days of employment |
| Internet stipend | $50 per month | From the first full month, with a monthly internet bill |

The home-office stipend is for a desk, chair, monitor or similar equipment. Home-office
equipment is paid from the stipend and cannot also be claimed through the expense
process. A laptop and its accessories are provided by IT and do not use the stipend.

## 4. Working from another country

An employee may work from another country for up to **20 working days** per calendar
year. The request needs manager approval at least **30 days** before the trip, and People
Operations must confirm that the destination is not on the restricted list (tax,
immigration and data-protection rules). Working abroad for longer than 20 days needs an
approved relocation and is not covered by this policy.

## 5. Security while remote

Employees work only on the company laptop and use the company VPN when they are on an
untrusted network. Screens must be locked when the employee steps away. Customer data may
not be printed or stored on personal devices. See the Information Security Policy and the
Acceptable Use Policy.

## 6. Changing work model

A change of work model needs the manager's agreement and notice to People Operations at
least 30 days ahead. Moving more than 50 miles from an office requires a conversation
about the work model before the move.
