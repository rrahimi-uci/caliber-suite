# Leave and Time Off Policy

Document ID: POL-HR-003
Policy domain: hr
Owner: People Operations
Version: 4.1
Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy covers paid and unpaid time off for full-time employees. Part-time employees
receive entitlements pro rata to their contracted hours. Contractors are not covered.

## 2. Paid time off (PTO)

PTO accrues monthly. Employees with less than 5 years of service receive **20 days** a
year; employees with 5 or more years of service receive **25 days** a year. New joiners
accrue from their start date, and PTO may be used as it accrues.

PTO requests of up to 5 consecutive days need manager approval at least 5 business days
ahead. Longer requests need approval at least 20 business days ahead.

At the end of the year an employee may carry over at most **5 days**. Carried-over days
must be used by **31 March**; after that date they expire without payment. When
employment ends, accrued and unused PTO is paid out where local law requires it.

## 3. Public holidays

The company observes 11 public holidays per year, listed on the intranet by country. A
public holiday that falls inside a PTO period does not use a PTO day.

## 4. Sick leave

Employees have **10 paid sick days** a year. A medical certificate is required after
**3 consecutive days** of sickness. Sick days do not carry over. An employee who is
unwell must tell their manager before the start of their working day.

## 5. Parental leave

| Leave type | Paid duration | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks at full pay | Births and adoptions on or after 2026-01-01 |
| Secondary caregiver | 8 weeks at full pay | Births and adoptions on or after 2026-01-01 |

Version 4.1 raised parental leave from the 12 weeks (primary) and 6 weeks (secondary)
granted by version 4.0. Employees whose child was born or placed before 1 January 2026
keep the version 4.0 entitlement.

Parental leave may be taken in one block or in up to two blocks within the first 12
months after the birth or placement. Employees must give 8 weeks' notice where possible.

## 6. Bereavement and other leave

- **Bereavement.** 5 paid days for the death of a spouse, partner, child, parent or
  sibling, and 2 paid days for the death of other relatives.
- **Jury duty and legal summons.** Paid for the full period, with proof of the summons.
- **Unpaid leave.** Up to 90 days, with the approval of a director. Benefits continue
  for the first 30 days.

## 7. Recording leave

All leave is recorded in the HR system by the employee and approved by the manager.
Unrecorded absence is treated as unauthorised and is handled under the performance
policy.
