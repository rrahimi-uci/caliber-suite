# Travel and Expense Policy

Document ID: POL-FIN-002
Policy domain: finance
Owner: Finance Operations
Version: 3.0
Effective date: 2026-07-01
Supersedes: version 2.5 (effective 2024-04-01)

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets out what business travel and expenses CALIBER will pay for, the limits
that apply, and how to claim. It applies to all employees and contractors who are
authorised to travel.

## 2. Changes in version 3.0

Version 3.0 replaces the limits of version 2.5 for travel that starts on or after
**1 July 2026**: the hotel limit rose from $180 to $220 per night, and the domestic daily
meal allowance rose from $65 to $75. Travel that started before 1 July 2026 is reimbursed
at the version 2.5 limits.

## 3. Limits

| Expense | Limit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotel | $220 per night | Tier 1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London): $300 per night |
| Meals, domestic | $75 per day | Per diem, no receipts needed for meals |
| Meals, international | $90 per day | Per diem, no receipts needed for meals |
| Ground transport | Actual cost | Choose the most economical suitable option |
| Mileage (personal car) | $0.70 per mile | Record the start and end of each trip |

## 4. Air travel

Book economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer,
premium economy is allowed. Business class needs approval from a Vice President before
booking. Book at least 14 days ahead where possible. Use the company travel portal.

## 5. Approvals

| Total trip cost | Approver |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $2,500 | Employee's manager |
| Above $2,500 | Director, before booking |
| International travel | Director, before booking |

Expenses that are not travel (software, equipment, subscriptions) follow the Vendor and
Procurement Policy.

## 6. Claims and receipts

Submit an expense report within **30 days** of the end of the trip or the date of the
expense. A receipt is required for any single expense over **$25**, apart from meal per
diems. Late claims need approval from the Director of Finance Operations.

Alcohol is reimbursable only at client meals and is capped at 25% of the meal total.
Fines, personal entertainment, upgrades for personal benefit and mini-bar charges are not
reimbursable. Home-office equipment is not claimed here; it is covered by the home-office
stipend in the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy.

## 7. Payment

Approved claims are paid with the next payroll run, at the latest 15 business days after
approval.
