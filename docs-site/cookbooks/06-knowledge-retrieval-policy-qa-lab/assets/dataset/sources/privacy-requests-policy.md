# Privacy Requests Policy

Document ID: POL-PRV-002
Policy domain: privacy
Owner: Privacy Office
Version: 1.3
Effective date: 2026-02-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains how CALIBER handles requests from individuals who want to exercise
their data protection rights: access to their personal data, correction, deletion,
restriction, objection and portability. It applies to personal data of customers, their
end users, job applicants and employees.

## 2. Receiving a request

A request can arrive through the privacy form, by e-mail to privacy@caliber.example, or
through any employee. Any employee who receives a request must forward it to the Privacy
Office the same business day and must not respond to it themselves.

The Privacy Office logs the request, acknowledges it within **5 business days**, and
checks the identity of the requester before releasing or changing any data. Identity is
verified by confirming the e-mail address on file and, for access and deletion requests,
one additional factor. A request made on someone's behalf needs written authority.

## 3. Response deadlines

The Privacy Office responds to a verified request within **30 calendar days** of
receiving it. For a complex request, or when many requests arrive together, the deadline
can be extended by up to **60 further days**. The requester must be told about the
extension, and the reason for it, within the first 30 days.

The 30-day clock stops while the Privacy Office waits for identity verification or for
clarification from the requester.

## 4. Fees

CALIBER does not charge for a request. If a request is manifestly unfounded or
excessive, for example repeated identical requests within a month, the Privacy Office may
refuse it or charge a fee of $25 to cover administrative cost. The Privacy Office, not
the employee, takes that decision and records the reason.

## 5. Specific rights

- **Access.** The requester receives a copy of their personal data and the purposes for
  which it is used.
- **Deletion.** Data is deleted unless a legal hold or a legal duty to keep it applies.
  Backups are not searched; the data leaves backups on the normal backup schedule.
- **Portability.** Data that the requester provided is exported as JSON or CSV.
- **Correction.** Inaccurate data is corrected in all systems within the response
  deadline.

## 6. Refusals

A request may be refused, in whole or in part, where the law allows it: for example, when
the data is under a legal hold or when releasing it would reveal another person's
personal data. A refusal must be explained in writing and must tell the requester they can
complain to their data protection authority.

## 7. Personal data breaches

A breach of personal data is handled as a security incident under the Information
Security Policy. The Privacy Office notifies the data protection authority within
**72 hours** of becoming aware of a breach that is likely to harm individuals, and notifies
the affected individuals without undue delay when the risk to them is high.
