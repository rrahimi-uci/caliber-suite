# Vendor and Procurement Policy

Document ID: POL-FIN-009
Policy domain: finance
Owner: Finance Operations and Legal
Version: 1.5
Effective date: 2025-11-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs how CALIBER buys goods and services and who may commit the company to
a purchase. It applies to every purchase, including software subscriptions and
consultants, paid by card, invoice or expense claim.

## 2. Competitive quotes

A purchase of up to $10,000 per year needs no competing quote. A purchase above $10,000 and
up to $25,000 per year needs a written justification. A purchase above **$25,000** per year
needs at least **three** competing quotes, unless the Director of Finance Operations
approves a single-source purchase in writing.

## 3. Security and privacy review

A vendor must pass a security review before use if it will process or store customer data,
connect to CALIBER systems, or receive more than **$10,000** in a year. The business owner
opens the review through the procurement portal. The Security team completes the review
within **10 business days** of receiving a complete questionnaire.

A vendor that processes personal data must also sign a data processing agreement before
any data is shared. Approved vendors are reviewed again every 12 months.

## 4. Signing authority

Only the people below may sign a contract. Signing authority cannot be delegated further.

| Annual contract value | Who may sign |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $25,000 | Director |
| Above $25,000, up to $100,000 | Vice President |
| Above $100,000, up to $500,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Above $500,000 | Chief Executive Officer |

Contracts that include an automatic renewal, an unlimited liability clause or a
termination fee need review by Legal regardless of value.

## 5. Purchase orders and payment

A purchase order is required for any purchase above $5,000. Invoices are paid 30 days after
receipt, if the goods or services were delivered and the invoice matches the purchase
order. Do not pay a vendor invoice that has no purchase order.

## 6. Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose any personal or family interest in a vendor to their manager and to
Finance Operations before the vendor is selected. An employee with such an interest takes
no part in the decision.

## 7. Software subscriptions on a card

Software subscriptions bought on a company card are subject to the same security review
thresholds as any other purchase. Using a card does not avoid the review.
