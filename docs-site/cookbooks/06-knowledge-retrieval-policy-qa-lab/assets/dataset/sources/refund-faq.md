# Billing FAQ for customers

Document ID: FAQ-BIL-001
Policy domain: billing
Owner: Support Enablement
Last reviewed: 2025-11-12

This page answers the questions customers ask us most about invoices and refunds. It is
published in the Help Center. If something here seems out of date, tell Support
Enablement so we can fix it.

## How long do I have to ask for a refund?

You can request a refund within **14 days** of the date of your charge. After 14 days
the charge is considered final and we cannot refund it.

## How do I get my money back?

Open the Billing portal, find the charge, and choose **Request refund**. Refunds go back
to the card you paid with and usually arrive in about a week.

## Do annual plans get prorated?

Yes. If you cancel an annual plan part way through the year, we refund the months you
have not used. Contact Support and we will calculate the amount.

## Are setup fees refundable?

No. One-time setup fees are non-refundable.

## Can I get a refund for usage-based overage charges?

No. Overage charges cover usage that has already happened, so they cannot be refunded.

## Why was I charged twice?

Duplicate charges are rare. If you see two identical charges on the same day, contact
Support with both invoice numbers and we will refund the duplicate.

## When will I get an invoice?

Invoices are e-mailed on the day of the charge to the billing contact on the account and
are also available under Account > Billing > Invoices for 24 months.

## Can I change the e-mail address that receives invoices?

Yes. Account owners can change the billing contact under Account > Billing > Contacts.
Changes take effect for the next invoice.

## What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Annual plans can also be paid by bank
transfer; contact Sales to set that up.
