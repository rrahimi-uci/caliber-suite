# Acceptable Use and Device Policy

Document ID: POL-IT-001
Policy domain: it
Owner: IT Operations
Version: 3.3
Effective date: 2026-04-01

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy tells employees and contractors how to use CALIBER devices, accounts and
networks. It complements the Information Security Policy; where the two overlap, follow
the stricter rule.

## 2. Company devices

Every employee receives a company laptop, managed by IT through device management. Laptops
are replaced on a **36-month** refresh cycle, or earlier if the device is faulty. Staff
must not remove or disable the management agent, the disk encryption or the endpoint
protection software.

Screens lock automatically after **5 minutes** of inactivity. USB storage devices are
blocked by default; IT can grant an exception for a named person and a stated purpose.

## 3. Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen device to the IT Service Desk within **4 hours** of discovering
the loss, day or night, using the 24-hour hotline. IT locks and wipes the device remotely.
A lost device is also a suspected security incident, so IT forwards the report to
Security Engineering, which follows the incident process in the Information Security
Policy. The employee does not need to file a second report.

## 4. Personal devices

Personal phones and tablets may be used for e-mail and chat only after they are enrolled
in mobile device management, which requires a screen-lock code and allows IT to remove
company data. Personal devices must never be used to access production systems or to
store customer data. Personal laptops are not allowed to connect to the company network.

## 5. Software

Install software only from the approved catalog. Requests for other software go through
the IT portal and need a business reason; a security review may be required. Pirated or
unlicensed software is prohibited.

## 6. Passwords and accounts

Use the company password manager for every work credential. Never share a password, even
with IT staff. MFA, as required by the Information Security Policy, must be enabled on
every account that supports it.

## 7. Acceptable and personal use

Company systems exist for company work. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not
interfere with work, does not break the law and does not involve storing personal files
of more than a few megabytes. Never use company systems to harass others, to run a
personal business, or to visit illegal or sexually explicit sites.

## 8. Monitoring

CALIBER monitors company systems for security and compliance. Monitoring covers
metadata and security events, and content only where the law permits and Legal approves.
Employees should expect no privacy for personal content stored on company systems.

## 9. Leaving the company

All access is revoked by the end of the last working day. The laptop and accessories are
returned within 5 business days; IT then wipes the device.
